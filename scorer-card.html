1 Signing in

Your name and the password are both keys. Neither works without the other, so write your name here exactly as the director has it:

  1. Name:
  2. Password:

Capitals and punctuation do not matter; a different spelling does. If the list says Xu Shao, then xu shao works and Shao Xu does not.

Turned away? It will not say which half was wrong, on purpose. Check both, then find a director. Never sign in as somebody else — every sheet is recorded against the name you typed.

2 Reading a contestant ID

A  01  1
Adivision — A or B
01team number, always two digits
1which member of the team, 1 to 4

So B073 is division B, team 7, third member. Type it without spaces; the portal uppercases it for you.

3 Marking an answer sheet

  1. Type the ID. The contestant's name appears by itself — if it does not, they are not on the participant list; carry on anyway.
  2. Type the twenty answers. Tab or Enter moves along. You can paste a whole row at once.
  3. Check the name on screen matches the name on the paper.
  4. Save. The toast tells you the ID and the score it worked out.

Marked one wrong? Type the ID again — the paper comes back exactly as you left it, ready to correct.

Notes

4 Blank, zero and unreadable

  • Zero is a real answer. Type 0. It can be right.
  • Nothing written? Leave the box empty. Empty is not the same as wrong, and the portal counts them apart.
  • Can't read a digit? Do not guess. Leave it, and put the paper on the director's pile.
  • Answers are whole numbers, never negative. A red box means the portal could not read what you typed.

5 The guts round

  1. Choose the division and team, then the set. The division picks the answer key; a team not on the list gets a warning — re-check the number on the sheet.
  2. Four answers per set. Save each set as you finish it.
  3. The projector board follows along by itself. It stops moving in the last ten minutes — that is the freeze, not a fault.

6 "Someone else has this sheet"

Two people cannot mark the same paper at once, so the portal holds one for whoever opened it first. If you see that message, move to another paper — it is not an error and nothing is lost.

A held sheet frees itself about two minutes after that person stops. You do not have to do anything.

7 When something looks wrong

  • "Lost the connection" — it reconnects on its own. Keep marking; your work is saved. Still stuck after a minute, reload the page.
  • The page says a newer version is available — reload it.
  • A save did not toast — it did not save. Check the ID and press Save again.
  • Anything else — tell a director. Do not open the Admin tab.

8 Two things that matter most

  1. The right ID. Everything else can be fixed later; a paper filed under the wrong contestant is the one mistake that is hard to find.
  2. Say so. A mistake you mention in ten seconds costs nothing. The same mistake found at the awards ceremony costs the ceremony.
